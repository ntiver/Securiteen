Social Media Ban for Under 16s

What You Need to Know

When did the ban start?

From 10 December 2025, children under the age of 16 will no longer be allowed to access or create accounts on major social media platforms in Australia.

Which platforms are affected?

The ban covers the most widely used platforms by young people, including:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- TikTok
- Snapchat
- X (Twitter)
- Reddit
- YouTube

Other current and future platforms with social networking features will also fall under the rules.

What are the age restrictions?

Children under 16: Not permitted to hold or use accounts.

Ages 16 and 17: May still access platforms, though restrictions and protections are likely to increase.

Parents and carers: Will play a key role in guiding, monitoring, and managing devices at home to ensure children comply.
